Before we start

This has happened before.

The notebook lasted eleven days. The app lasted three weeks, and it's still installed.

January lasted until the second week of February. You didn't decide to stop. You just stopped opening it, and then it had been a while, and then it was March.

You don't lack discipline. You use it every day at work, where other people are counting on you and would notice.

That's the whole difference. At work, someone notices.

Alone, nothing notices. You can write I need to sort my space out six months running and no one will ever put those six months side by side and ask what the sixth one cost.

You didn't need more resolve. You needed a witness.

The Work

Four months. One pillar at a time.

You can't rebuild four domains at once. Anyone telling you otherwise is selling enthusiasm. Each month belongs to one pillar. The other three keep being tracked, and when one starts contradicting the others, we deal with it.

Month One
Body
The most measurable and the least deniable. Condition and capability. Inside three weeks you'll know whether you're doing the work, and so will I.
Month Two
Order
Your space, your presentation, your attention. Screen time and calendar as a measure of self-governance. The month people are most surprised by.
Month Three
Responsibility
Your duties and your people, named and looked at directly. The hardest month. It comes third because by then we know each other.
Month Four
Mastery
One pursuit. A vision specific enough to disqualify most of what you currently do. Sessions, stages, and a contract you sign.
The Cadence

Eight calls. Sixteen check-ins.

  • A call every fortnight.Forty-five minutes, twice a month, on that month's pillar. Eight across the four months.
  • A written check-in every week.Three lines by email, same day every week. It takes under a minute.
  • A reply every time.Short, on purpose. The value isn't weekly coaching. It's that a person read it, will remember it next week, and will ask.
  • The app underneath it all.Your scores and your own words from three months ago, sitting next to what you actually did.
The weekly check-in
  1. What did you commit to?
  2. Did you do it?
  3. If not, one line on why.

Same three questions, same day, every week for sixteen weeks. It's deliberately short. The point isn't the writing. The point is that it goes to someone.

What it costs

We settle that when we talk. It's paid for the four months rather than by the month — you're not renting access to me. You're committing to an arc with an end, and paying at the start is the first thing you're held to.

I'm running this with a small number of people for the first time, at a rate that reflects it. In exchange I'll ask you at the end to tell me plainly what worked and what didn't.

Fit

This isn't for everyone.

It's for you if

  • You're functioning. The problem isn't crisis, it's drift.
  • You've tried to fix this alone more than once.
  • You want the truth about the gap between what you say and what you do, from someone with the record in front of him.
  • You'd rather be asked a hard question than handed a technique.

It isn't if

  • You're in crisis and need clinical support. This isn't therapy and I'm not qualified to provide it.
  • You want encouragement. There's a great deal of that available, and most of it is free.
  • You want to be asked how you feel about your goals rather than shown what you did about them.

Overview

Remoralization is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

  • Account information: email address used to create your account
  • Health data: body measurements, waist measurements, and run data you manually enter
  • Photos: condition check-in photos and house spot photos you take within the app
  • Usage data: your ratings, goals, reflections, and progress entries

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the app's features and track your progress across the four pillars
  • To generate personalized feedback from the archetype system
  • Your data is never sold to third parties

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase. Photos are stored in Supabase Storage.

AI Features

The app uses Anthropic's Claude API to generate archetype responses. Your pillar data is sent to this API to generate responses. No personally identifiable information is included.
